Featured projects

Selected engineering work

Four pieces of work that show how the same underlying discipline — ground behaviour interpretation, data-led decision making, and cross-team coordination — plays out across a crisis response, a materials-engineering problem, an academic research project and an operational cost initiative.

01Nexa Resources

Extremo Norte access ramp rehabilitation

Vazante Mine, Brazil · March – August 2021

When ground displacement was identified in the mine's only personnel access and emergency egress route, she led the technical response — interpreting the cracking and its likely cause, directing a multi-instrument monitoring programme (crack meters, extensometers, laser points, inclinometers), and managing the full operational execution of a five-stage rehabilitation combining cable bolting, shotcrete, spiling and steel arch sets across several hundred metres of underground access. She took on sole geotechnical accountability for the area partway through the project and coordinated directly with operations to keep the programme progressing without halting wider mine production.

Access route fully restored, August 2021
02Nexa Resources

Shotcrete re-entry time optimisation

Vazante Mine, Brazil

Reviewed and redesigned the mine's steel fibre-reinforced shotcrete system end to end — cement selection, admixture dosing, laboratory characterisation, full-scale batching trials and field strength verification with penetrometer readings underground — to establish a mix and control system that reached adequate early strength faster, without compromising ground support performance.

Re-entry time cut from 6 to 3 hours
03University of São Paulo

Stope dilution prediction calibration

Master's thesis, using Vazante Mine data (2019–2022)

Built a site-specific statistical correction for the industry-standard Mathews-Potvin stability graph, using more than 400 cavity-monitoring surveys across cable-bolted, non-cable-bolted and self-supported stopes. Linear regression and correlation analysis confirmed the graph performs well once reinforcement is installed, but systematically overestimates dilution in unsupported ground — and the calibration equations she derived meaningfully improved prediction accuracy.

~20% lower prediction error overall, up to 29% in unsupported ground
